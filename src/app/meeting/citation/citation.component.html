Signal Methodology

How the recommendation signal is calculated

1 Market Outlook
20%

Measures the current outlook for the client's sector.

Increase Neutral Decrease
2 Theme Sentiment
30%

Measures sentiment from relevant themes matched to the client.

Positive = +1 Mixed / Neutral = 0 Negative = -1
3 SectorTag Sentiment
50%

Measures sentiment from sector tag.

Positive = +1 Mixed / Neutral = 0 Negative = -1
Final Signal Score
Outlook × 20% + Theme × 30% + SectorTag × 50%
